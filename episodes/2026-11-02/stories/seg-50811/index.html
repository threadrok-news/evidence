Full Narration

South Africa's $1.7 billion in annual exports to the United States are now at risk. The Trump administration is suspending both South Africa and Rwanda from AGOA, the duty-free trade initiative that has underpinned African export growth for decades. The administration cited rule violations under the AGOA framework. Rwanda's defence ministry was also sanctioned by the US Treasury, a move that came barely a week before today's trade announcement. For the millions of workers whose livelihoods depend on those export chains, the suspension takes effect with immediate consequence. The signal to watch is whether either government moves to negotiate reinstatement terms, or whether these suspensions harden into something longer-term.
