Full Narration

12 B-1 bombers are no longer at RAF Fairford. The U.S. Air Force evacuated them from the British base over the weekend after British security forces uncovered an alleged Iranian-linked terror plot targeting the facility. 5 British men were arrested near the base. Axios reports that several of those detained hold dual British-Iranian citizenship and are suspected of planning a direct attack on the airbase. RAF Fairford has been a staging point for U.S. strategic bomber operations. The decision to pull the aircraft signals how seriously both governments are treating the threat assessment. Watch for any announcement on whether the bombers return or are repositioned elsewhere in Europe.
