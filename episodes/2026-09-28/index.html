Full Narration

Here's what matters: the platform functions as a set of security layers around AI systems, enforcing limits on what those systems can do or access. Axios reports the tool is already deployed, not planned for future release. The question is whether adoption will be broad enough to matter. The platform is open, meaning any developer can integrate it. The test is whether the industry treats it as a standard or an option.
