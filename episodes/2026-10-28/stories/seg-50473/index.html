Flávio Bolsonaro Arrives in Miami After Election Loss

2026-10-28 7 outlets SRQ
0.76 V2×27
7 articles · SRQ 0.757

Defeated Brazilian presidential candidate Flávio Bolsonaro flew to Miami with his family after authorities ordered him to surrender his passport, denying he fled the country.

Full Narration

Flávio Bolsonaro arrived in Miami with his family days after losing a presidential election in Brazil. Brazilian authorities had ordered him to surrender his passport. He did not. He's now on U.S. soil, and the White House, according to NPR, gave its blessing for his arrival. Bolsonaro is denying that the move amounts to fleeing Brazil, calling the trip pre-planned. Brazilian authorities issued the passport surrender order as part of ongoing legal proceedings against him. The question now is whether Brazil formally requests his return and how the U.S. responds. His father, former Brazilian president Jair Bolsonaro, is separately facing legal jeopardy in Brazil. The family's presence in the U.S. adds a diplomatic dimension that neither government has fully addressed on the record.
