Appeals Court Blocks Trump Order Keeping Michigan Coal Plant Open

A federal appeals court unanimously struck down the Trump administration's emergency directive forcing a Michigan coal plant to remain open past its planned retirement date.

Full Narration

A federal appeals court ruled 3-0 against the Trump administration Friday, striking down the use of emergency powers to keep a coal-fired power plant in Michigan running past its scheduled retirement date. The court found the Department of Energy acted outside its legal authority. The plant had been ordered to stay open under an emergency directive last year. The D.C. Circuit's unanimous ruling removes that order. Here's the context: the Energy Department's emergency authority exists to manage grid stability during crises, and the court found that standard was not met in this case. The test now is whether the administration appeals to the Supreme Court or allows the plant to proceed with retirement. The ruling sets a precedent on how far presidential emergency powers extend into energy infrastructure decisions.
