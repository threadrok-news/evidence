0.75 V2×16

The Supreme Court refused to block a lower-court ruling against Trump's mail-in ballot executive order, leaving midterm election rules intact.

Full Narration

Mail-in ballot rules for the midterm elections will stay as they are. The Supreme Court blocked the Trump administration's executive order that sought to restrict mail-in voting, refusing to overturn a lower court ruling that had already struck the order down. For voters and election administrators planning for November, that's a settled question, for now. Trump responded publicly, slamming the court for the decision. The administration had argued the restrictions were necessary for election integrity. The lower court disagreed, and the Supreme Court declined to intervene on appeal. Pay attention to whether the administration pursues any alternative legal or legislative path before the midterm filing deadlines close.
