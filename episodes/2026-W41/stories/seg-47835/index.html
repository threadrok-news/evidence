Full Narration

Hundreds of protesters affiliated with India's so-called Cockroach movement were detained in Delhi ahead of a planned Saturday demonstration. Thousands of security personnel were deployed across the capital. Leaders from the movement have since been released from custody, according to BBC News. The protests center on a striking claim: more than 130 million voters have been removed from India's electoral rolls since last year. That figure is corroborated but its implications are actively disputed. Pay attention to whether Delhi authorities respond formally to the voter roll question, because that's the number driving the demonstrations.
