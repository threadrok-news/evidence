Full Narration

The jury in the Lindsay Clancy murder trial told the judge Wednesday it cannot agree on a verdict. This is the second time the jury has declared itself deadlocked. Clancy is accused of killing her 3 children in Duxbury, Massachusetts in 2023. Her defense argued postpartum psychosis left her without control over her actions at the time of the killings. The prosecution disputed that. The jury has now twice been unable to resolve that central question. The judge must now decide whether to declare a mistrial or issue further instructions and send jurors back to deliberate. PBS NewsHour reports no ruling on next steps had been issued as of Wednesday afternoon.
