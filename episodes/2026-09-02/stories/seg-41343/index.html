The U.S. Mint released 250,000 $1 coins featuring President Trump's image, selling out within hours at $61 per roll of 25.

Full Narration

250,000 $1 coins bearing President Trump's likeness went on sale Wednesday afternoon — and sold out within hours. A roll of 25 costs $61. The U.S. Mint confirmed the release, and The Hill reports this is the first time in nearly 200 years that a living person has appeared on American currency. The last precedent dates back to the early 19th century. What this means practically is that collectors, not the general economy, are the primary market — the face value is $1, but demand immediately outpaced supply at the commemorative price. The question worth watching is whether additional mint runs follow. The initial 250,000-coin release is already gone, and no announcement on restocking has been made as of Wednesday evening.
