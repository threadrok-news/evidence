Full Narration

The US Postal Service published a final rule Friday restricting mail-in voting practices — despite 2 separate court orders currently blocking those changes from taking effect. The rule was issued anyway, in compliance with a March executive order from President Trump. The Hill confirmed the USPS moved forward with publication even after courts intervened. The rule is on the books, but enforcement remains frozen while legal challenges proceed. The test is whether the courts extend those blocks or allow implementation before the next federal election cycle. Both the executive action and the judicial response are now formally on record.
