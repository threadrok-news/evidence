Trump Removes 10% Tariff on Irish Whiskey

2026-09-13 6 outlets SRQ
0.74 V1×6 V2×7 V4×3

President Trump announced the removal of the U.S.'s 10% tariff on Irish whiskey, a targeted trade concession affecting one of Ireland's key exports.

Full Narration

Starting today, Irish whiskey enters the U.S. market without a 10% tariff. President Trump announced the removal of that duty, a levy that had been in place and affecting one of Ireland's most significant exports to American consumers. The Hill confirmed the announcement. The tariff cut applies specifically to Irish whiskey — not a broader trade package, just this single category. What to watch is whether this becomes a template for other targeted trade concessions, or whether it stays a one-off. For now, the price at the shelf is the most immediate consequence — importers and retailers will decide how quickly that 10% flows through.
