Full Narration

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has now passed 4,600 confirmed cases and killed more than 2,100 people. Those numbers, corroborated across 5 outlets, make this the deadliest Ebola outbreak in the country's recorded history. Cases are rising at a pace that outstrips every previous outbreak in the DRC. The case fatality rate implied by confirmed figures sits near 46%. The outbreak has not been contained. What the sourcing does not yet establish is the geographic spread of new cases or the current status of international response teams on the ground. The known facts are severe: a record death toll, a record case count, and a trajectory that has not reversed.
