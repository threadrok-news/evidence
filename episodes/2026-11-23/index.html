CrowdStrike has agreed to acquire cloud security firm Zscaler in an all-stock deal valued at approximately $43.9 billion, pending regulatory approval.

Full Narration

$43.9 billion. That's what CrowdStrike is paying to absorb Zscaler in an all-stock deal that would reshape the cybersecurity landscape. Under the terms, Zscaler shareholders receive 1.135 CrowdStrike shares for each share they hold. TechCrunch reports the enterprise value sits at approximately $43.9 billion, making this one of the largest pure-play cybersecurity acquisitions on record. The deal combines CrowdStrike's endpoint protection with Zscaler's cloud-based network security platform. Here's what matters for anyone watching the security software sector: two of the dominant cloud-native players are consolidating under a single roof. The deal still requires regulatory clearance before it closes.
