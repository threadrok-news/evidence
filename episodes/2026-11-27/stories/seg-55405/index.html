A US military aircraft fired on and disabled the M/V Pacific Archer in the Gulf of Oman after the vessel reportedly attempted to breach a US naval blockade of Iranian ports.

Full Narration

A US military aircraft fired precision munitions at a cargo vessel in the Gulf of Oman on Thursday, disabling its propulsion. The ship is the M/V Pacific Archer. US Central Command says it attempted to breach a naval blockade of Iranian ports. The strike left the ship immobile in the water. The blockade itself is a significant escalation in the ongoing US posture toward Iran. A US naval cordon around Iranian ports is now an active operational reality in the region. Pay attention to whether other vessels test the blockade in the coming days. The Pacific Archer incident is the first publicly confirmed enforcement action of this kind, and it sets a clear precedent for how the US intends to enforce it.
