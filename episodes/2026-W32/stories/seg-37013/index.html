Full Narration

Iran and Oman reached an agreement on shipping coordinates through the Strait of Hormuz this week. The deal sets a navigable route for commercial vessels through one of the world's most contested chokepoints. No formal agreement between the United States and Iran was announced. But Washington and Tehran are still in talks, and the Oman deal is being read in diplomatic circles as a signal that the broader negotiation has not collapsed. Separately, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Pakistan signed a mutual defense agreement modeled on NATO's Article 5. The question now is whether the Hormuz arrangement holds while U.S.-Iran diplomacy continues at a pace neither side has publicly defined.
