The CDC declared this week that the largest cyclosporiasis outbreak on record is over. 12,883 people were sickened across 21 states since May 1st. 2 people died in Michigan. The outbreak was tied to shredded iceberg lettuce grown by Taylor Farms in Mexico. That linkage is corroborated by multiple outlets and verified independently. The scale, 12,883 cases, makes this the largest cyclosporiasis outbreak the CDC has recorded. The declaration that it's over is the CDC's official position. For anyone who experienced unexplained gastrointestinal illness this summer across those 21 states, that sourcing trail now points to a specific product and a specific grower.
