Australia plans to require social media platforms to notify all users and offer a choice to opt in or out of personalised, algorithm-driven content feeds.

Full Narration

Every Australian on social media — new and existing users — would receive a notification giving them a direct choice over how their feed is curated. Australia is moving to force platforms to let users over 16 opt in or out of personalised algorithms, and the government issued a formal statement on that plan Tuesday. Here's what matters: this isn't a voluntary industry move. Platforms would be required to send the notification. Users would get to decide whether an algorithm shapes what they see, or whether they get a chronological or default feed instead. The requirement covers both new sign-ups and people already on the platforms. Watch for how major platforms respond — compliance timelines and implementation details haven't been spelled out publicly yet.
