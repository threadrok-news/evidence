Full Narration

More than 200,000 people have fled or been ordered to shelter in place as wildfires tear through Spain and France. Spain declared a national emergency around Madrid, where at least 2 fires merged into a single front. Evacuations account for 150,000 of those displaced. BBC News confirmed the national emergency declaration and the merged fire fronts in the Madrid region. The scale of the evacuations puts this among the largest wildfire responses Europe has seen in recent years. The signal to watch is whether the merged fire system near Madrid can be contained before it pushes further into populated areas. Conditions driving the fires have not been reported as improving.
