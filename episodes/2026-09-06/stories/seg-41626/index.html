Full Narration

5 people are dead after an Amazon cargo plane overran the runway at Miami airport around 2 p.m. Saturday. 5 more were seriously injured. BBC News reports the aircraft went off the end of the runway and came to rest beyond the airport perimeter. The crash triggered an emergency response at Miami International. Amazon has not yet issued a public statement on the cause. Federal investigators are expected to open a formal inquiry into the incident. The question now is what the flight data and cockpit recorder show. Those findings will determine whether the cause was mechanical, procedural, or related to runway conditions.
