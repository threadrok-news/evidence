Full Narration

Boston Scientific disclosed a global disruption caused by an ongoing cyberattack, TechCrunch reports. The company confirmed the attack caused a global network disruption affecting operations across multiple regions. Boston Scientific makes cardiac devices and medical equipment used in hospitals worldwide. The company hasn't detailed which systems are affected or whether patient data was compromised. What matters here is the operational scope: a global network disruption at a major medical device manufacturer carries direct implications for hospital supply chains and device management systems. No recovery timeline has been publicly confirmed.
