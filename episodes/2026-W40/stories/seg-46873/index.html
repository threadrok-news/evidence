Tennessee's execution of convicted murderer Christa Pike was halted Wednesday after two doses of anesthetic failed to cause death, the second failed execution in the state this year.

Full Narration

Tennessee attempted to execute Christa Pike on Wednesday. It did not succeed. Two doses of anesthetic were administered. Pike survived both. Pike was convicted of murder. Wednesday's failed execution is the second time this year Tennessee has been unable to carry out a scheduled lethal injection. The state halted the procedure after the two doses failed to produce the intended effect. BBC News confirmed the execution was halted Wednesday. What Tennessee's corrections department has not yet announced is a rescheduled date or a review of the protocol used.
