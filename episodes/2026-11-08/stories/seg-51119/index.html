Full Narration

Nvidia's board has opened a formal investigation into CEO Jensen Huang after he admitted to a romantic relationship with a woman suspected of passing information to Beijing. The board announced the probe after reports of the affair became public. Huang has acknowledged the relationship and confirmed he was unfaithful to his wife. The woman at the center of this is suspected of spying for China, though Nvidia has not detailed what the investigation will examine or how long it will run. Here's what matters for anyone watching the semiconductor sector: Nvidia sits at the center of U.S. export controls on advanced chips to China. A CEO entanglement with a suspected Chinese intelligence contact puts the company under a level of scrutiny that goes well beyond a typical corporate misconduct review. The board's next move is the signal to track.
