Supermarket freezers across 10 countries in Europe have gone dark after hackers raised their temperatures remotely. Retailers have been forced to discard food as a result. The attack hit refrigeration systems simultaneously across the continent, affecting food suppliers in the UK as well. Here's what matters: this wasn't a data breach. It was a physical consequence delivered through a digital vector. Frozen supply chains, discarded stock, and 10 countries affected in a single coordinated operation. BBC News confirmed the attack reached supermarkets across Europe. The scale suggests the targeting of shared refrigeration management software, though the specific entry point has not been publicly identified.
