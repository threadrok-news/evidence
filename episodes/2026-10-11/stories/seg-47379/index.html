Full Narration

Russia and Ukraine have agreed to stop targeting energy infrastructure immediately. That's the claim President Trump made this weekend, announcing what he's calling an 'energy ceasefire' covering oil refineries, power plants, and other critical facilities. The announcement came with a direct contradiction already on the table. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy stated that Russia was actively hitting energy infrastructure in Kyiv at the same time Trump was making the claim. The two sides issued directly conflicting accounts of what is happening on the ground. Here's what matters: no independent verification of a halt in strikes had been confirmed at the time of broadcast. The question now is whether battlefield conduct in the coming days reflects what the White House announced.
