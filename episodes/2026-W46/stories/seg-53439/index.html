Full Narration

Democrat Scott Colom won Mississippi's U.S. Senate race this week, defeating Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith. Colom is the first Black U.S. senator from Mississippi since the Reconstruction era, verified by multiple fact-checkers. Mississippi has not sent a Black senator to Washington since the post-Civil War Reconstruction period — a gap of more than 140 years. Colom's win flips a Senate seat in a state that has voted Republican in presidential elections for decades. The question now is what this result signals for Democratic competitiveness in deep-South states ahead of the next election cycle. Colom will fill a full Senate term.
