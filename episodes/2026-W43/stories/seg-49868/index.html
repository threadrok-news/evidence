Elon Musk confirmed he personally ordered speed caps on Starshield, the military version of Starlink, limiting Ukrainian hardware's connectivity during the ongoing war.

Full Narration

Elon Musk personally ordered a slowdown of Starlink connectivity for Ukrainian military hardware during the war. He capped the speeds at which Ukrainian devices could stay connected to the satellite network, and he's now confirmed that decision publicly. The affected service was Starshield, the military-grade version of Starlink. Musk's acknowledgment puts on record that a private individual unilaterally restricted battlefield communications for a country at war, according to BBC News. The implications for sovereign military dependence on private infrastructure are concrete. Ukraine has relied on Starlink as a primary communications backbone. The question now is what oversight, if any, governs a single company's ability to throttle access during active conflict.
