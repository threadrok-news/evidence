Elon Musk, CEO of both companies, has confirmed the boards of SpaceX and Tesla are exploring a potential merger, with no terms or timeline disclosed.

Full Narration

SpaceX is a private rocket company. Tesla is a public automaker. The boards of both are now exploring a merger — and the man pushing it runs both. Elon Musk has confirmed he's the architect of these discussions, calling the combination a good idea. What's structurally unusual here is that Musk sits on both sides of any negotiation. He's CEO of Tesla, he controls SpaceX, and he owns equity in each. The question worth tracking is how independent board oversight functions when the same person is effectively on both sides of the table. No deal terms have been disclosed, and no timeline has been set. The boards are described as exploring, not deciding. Watch for any formal merger committee announcements — those would signal the talks have moved past the idea stage.
