Distributed Denial of Secrets published personal records for more than 41,000 DHS employees on October 28, following a hack of the department's data.

Full Narration

More than 41,000 Department of Homeland Security employee records were published online Wednesday. The dataset went live on October 28, released by Distributed Denial of Secrets — a transparency collective that has published leaked government data before. Personal information on thousands of federal employees, out in the open. Here's what matters: this isn't a suspected breach still under investigation. The data is already published. DHS hasn't detailed what specific fields were exposed, but the record count is confirmed. The Verge reported the release the same day it went live. Watch for whether DHS issues guidance to affected employees or moves to have the dataset removed. The question isn't whether the leak happened — it did. The question is what 41,000 federal workers are supposed to do about it now.
