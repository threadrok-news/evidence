Full Narration

After the $6.3 billion breach, Binance users pulled $3.6 billion in withdrawals. The exchange has since resumed normal withdrawal operations and says it's covered 95% of the stolen assets — meaning it absorbed most of the loss rather than passing it to users. The question for Binance's users is whether the remaining 5% gap gets resolved, and on what timeline. Covering 95% of a $6.3 billion theft is a significant operational response. It doesn't close the story, but it does change the immediate stakes for account holders.
