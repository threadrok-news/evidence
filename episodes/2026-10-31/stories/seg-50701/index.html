Full Narration

Washington D.C. and Virginia are suing to stop construction of a presidential triumphal arch on the National Mall. The lawsuit names the National Parks Service and the Interior Department as defendants. Snopes has confirmed President Trump proposed the arch. U.S. District Court Judge Tanya Chutkan ordered both sides to negotiate a briefing schedule for a temporary restraining order by Saturday evening. That schedule will determine how quickly a court could block groundbreaking before construction begins. Pay attention to that Saturday deadline. If the two sides can't agree on a schedule, Chutkan will set one. A temporary restraining order, if granted, would halt any construction activity immediately. The legal fight is now on a fast clock.
