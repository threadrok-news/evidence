Far-right commentator Milo Yiannopoulos was deported from the U.S. to the UK on August 28 after ICE arrested him in New Orleans for overstaying his visa.

Full Narration

Milo Yiannopoulos was deported from the United States on August 28. ICE arrested him at Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport the day before, and by Friday he was back in the United Kingdom. The Department of Homeland Security confirmed the deportation to multiple outlets, citing an overstayed visa. What matters here is the profile of the person involved. Yiannopoulos built a public career as a vocal critic of U.S. immigration enforcement — and it's immigration enforcement that removed him. BBC News confirmed the deportation was carried out by ICE and that he was returned to his home country. The case is now with British authorities. No criminal charges have been publicly filed in the U.S. The question now is whether the UK takes any further action following his return.
