FDA Orders GLP-1 Makers to Add Vision Loss Warning

2026-11-10 4 outlets SRQ
0.41 V1×1 V2×16 V3×1

The FDA ordered makers of GLP-1 drugs, including Ozempic and Wegovy, to add label warnings about NAION, a rare condition that can cause permanent vision loss.

Full Narration

If you're taking a GLP-1 drug — Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro — the label is about to carry a new warning. The FDA has ordered manufacturers to add alerts about NAION, a rare eye condition that can cause sudden, permanent vision loss. The order applies across the GLP-1 drug class. NAION, non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy, cuts off blood flow to the optic nerve. It is rare, but it is irreversible. The FDA's move means patients and prescribers will now be formally notified of the risk before starting or continuing treatment, according to BBC News. Pay attention to whether prescribing rates or patient uptake shift in the months ahead. GLP-1 drugs are among the most widely used medications in the U.S. right now — the scale of that patient population makes even a rare side effect a significant public health signal.
