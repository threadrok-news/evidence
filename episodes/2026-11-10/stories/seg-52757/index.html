Jordan Elected House Minority Leader, Thune Leads Senate GOP

2026-11-10 6 outlets SRQ
0.72 V2×39 V3×3
6 articles · SRQ 0.719

House Republicans elected Jim Jordan as minority leader and Senate Republicans retained John Thune as their caucus leader for the next session.

Full Narration

House Republicans now have their leadership in place. Jim Jordan, the Ohio congressman and Judiciary Committee chair, was elected House minority leader on Tuesday. That puts him at the top of Republican House operations for the next session. On the Senate side, John Thune was elected as Senate Republican leader, a role that will carry through the final 2 years of President Trump's term. Thune and Jordan now anchor both chambers of Republican congressional leadership. Pay attention to how both men position themselves relative to the White House. Jordan has been among the most visible Trump allies in Congress. Thune's relationship with the administration has been more complicated. The dynamic between those 2 figures will shape the legislative calendar ahead.
