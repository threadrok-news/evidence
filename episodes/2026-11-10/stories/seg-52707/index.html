Full Narration

A U.S. Army soldier has been arrested near Gloucester, England, on suspicion of spying for Russia. The arrest involves a senior soldier — the specific rank has not been released — and British authorities are treating it as an espionage case with direct ties to Russian intelligence activity. The case marks a rare instance of a serving American military member being detained on allied soil for alleged foreign espionage. What matters here is jurisdiction: the soldier was arrested in the UK, meaning British law enforcement is leading the initial proceedings. No charges have been formally announced as of this broadcast. The U.S. Army has not publicly commented on the soldier's identity or assignment.
