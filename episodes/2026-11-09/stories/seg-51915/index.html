Supreme Court Clears Border Wall in Big Bend National Park

2026-11-09 9 outlets SRQ
0.86 V2×19 V3×1
9 articles · SRQ 0.864

The Supreme Court allowed the Trump administration to resume border wall construction in Texas's Big Bend National Park, overturning a lower court order that had paused the project.

Full Narration

Border wall construction is moving forward in Big Bend National Park in west Texas. The Supreme Court lifted a lower court's block on the project, allowing the Trump administration to continue building in one of the country's most remote protected landscapes. Here's the context: a federal judge had paused construction, and the administration went directly to the Supreme Court to override that pause. The court sided with the administration, clearing the way for work to continue through the Big Bend region, including areas inside the national park boundary. No final ruling on the underlying legal challenge has been issued. The signal to watch is whether the lower court's full hearing on the merits produces a different result once construction is already underway.
