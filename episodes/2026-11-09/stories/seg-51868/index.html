U.S. officials believe the woman Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang had an affair with was spying for China, raising national security concerns about the world's leading AI chip company.

Full Narration

The national security dimension of the Huang story goes further than a corporate governance dispute. The U.S. government believes the woman Huang had an affair with was actively spying for China, according to BBC News. Huang has admitted to the affair. The question now is what, if anything, was compromised at a company whose chips underpin the global AI industry. Nvidia controls the dominant share of the market for AI accelerator chips — hardware that both U.S. and Chinese technology sectors depend on. Intelligence officials flagged the relationship last year. The board investigation and the shareholder lawsuit are now running in parallel, with no resolution announced on either front.
