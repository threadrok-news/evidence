President Trump rejected an Iranian offer to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and resume nuclear talks within seven days, leaving the strait closed with no new negotiations scheduled.

Full Narration

The Strait of Hormuz remains closed after President Trump rejected an Iranian proposal to reopen it. Iran also offered to resume nuclear talks within 7 days. Trump turned down both offers. The strait carries roughly a fifth of the world's oil supply. Iran's proposal came with a 7-day timeline for nuclear negotiations. The rejection means that window has now closed, at least on Iran's original terms. The question now is whether either side returns to the table with a revised offer. The Financial Times reports no follow-up diplomatic contact has been announced. Both sides have issued conflicting public positions on what conditions would restart talks.
